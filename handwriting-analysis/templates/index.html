How it works

  1. Normalise — grayscale, denoise, Otsu binarise, crop to the ink, and fit to a common canvas.
  2. Measure — SSIM (structure), HOG cosine (stroke direction), ORB matches (local features), and geometry (ink density, aspect ratio, stroke count).
  3. Score — combine into a weighted 0–100 similarity with a plain-language verdict.
